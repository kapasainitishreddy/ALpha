References support general education, not jurisdiction-specific advice. NSE/CME/OIC contract terms are not interchangeable. The app's option and derivatives examples use simplified synthetic inputs. The Options page labels break-even as an expiry-only, pre-cost measure and gives a synthetic model case where pre-expiry option value rises while spot remains below that level. The European-style payoff tool does not reproduce Indian exercise, assignment, delivery or contract rules. The derivatives lab uses multiplier 1, synthetic units, a fixed ₹50,000 separate account, 0.03% fees plus 0.05% friction per gross leg, and simplified collateral/maintenance; none is current exchange contract sizing or margin. Live quote snapshots, where present elsewhere in the app, are distinct from simulated execution. The NIST reference supports general generative-AI risk management; the claim about the app's keyword demo is verified from its code, not inferred from NIST.
